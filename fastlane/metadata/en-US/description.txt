ATTO SOUND is a social audio platform that connects creators, families, and listeners through voice — the most human way to share who you are.

Whether you want to listen, follow your favorite voices, message in real time, or stay in contact with someone who matters, ATTO SOUND brings you closer through sound.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

LISTEN AND DISCOVER
Browse a personalized feed of audio posts and short-form video reels. Like, comment, share, and follow the voices that move you.

CREATE AND SHARE
Record audio posts, drop reels, and grow an audience that actually listens. Built-in tools for creators who want to publish without friction.

MESSAGE IN REAL TIME
Send text and voice notes to anyone you connect with. Conversations stay synced across your devices.

VOICE CALLS
Place high-quality VoIP calls to keep close contact, even across distance.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Support creators directly through subscription plans. Unlock advanced production tools, bridge numbers, and premium features.

THREE WAYS TO JOIN
• Listener — explore audio and follow creators
• Representative — stay connected with someone close
• Creator — build your audience and share your voice

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
You control who sees your content, who can message you, and who can call. Granular permissions for microphone, contacts, and notifications. Audio is encrypted in transit. Your data stays yours.

WHY ATTO SOUND
Voice carries what text can't — tone, intent, presence. ATTO SOUND is built for people who want a more human way to connect, follow creators, and stay close to who matters.
